Privacy Policy

My Secret Diary

Overview

My Secret Diary ("the App") is a personal journaling application. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices available regarding that information.

Your Diary Entries

Diary entries, moods, photos, and any content you write are stored locally on your device or browser. They are not uploaded to or read by us, and we do not have access to the contents of your entries.

Password / PIN Protection

Any password or PIN you set to lock the App is stored locally on your device to secure access to your entries. It is not transmitted to or stored on any external server.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on someone's prior visits.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

The App may use basic, aggregated analytics (such as page views) to understand overall usage patterns. This data does not include the content of your diary entries.

Data Storage & Security

Since entries and passwords are stored locally, clearing your browser data or uninstalling the App will permanently delete this information. We recommend using the App's backup feature, if available, before doing so.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the contact details provided on the App's store listing page.
